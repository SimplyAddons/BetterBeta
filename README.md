# Better Forever

**Better Forever** is a collection of quality-of-life, UI, and visual enhancements for WoW Forever.

## Features

- **Combo Points on Nameplates**  
  Shows your combo points on your target's nameplate (rogues, and druids in cat form).
- **Warlock Shard Reaper**  
  Deletes Soul Shards above a limit you set, through a Drain Soul macro, a bound key or a button, keeping your bags clear.
- **Wand Indicator**  
  Makes wand attacks easier to track: a bar to the next shot, a "fizzle..." when a shot is late, and fun comic-style effects like **ZAP!**, **POOF!**, and **SHAZAM!**
- **Mob Type Indicators**  
  Shows creature types such as Beast, Undead, Humanoid, Demon, etc, in tooltips and alongside nameplates with custom icons.
- **Cinematic Ultra Mode**  
  A one-click set of enhanced graphics settings (render scale, anti-aliasing, sharpening, shadows, water, draw distance, weather and spell effects) for a richer and more cinematic presentation. Your own settings are saved first and restored when you turn it off. In capital cities it switches to lighter settings for better FPS and comes back when you leave.
- **Automatic FPS Display**  
  Shows the FPS counter automatically whenever you log in or reload the UI.

## Options

All features are individually controlled and configurable under Interface > AddOns > Better Forever, or with `/bb` or `/bf`.

Better Forever is intended to be a growing collection of enhancements for WoW Forever.
